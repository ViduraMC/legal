Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Content Studio ("we") operates a private, self-hosted publishing tool ("the App") at https://postiz.taila5d151.ts.net. The App is used only by us to publish content we create to social media accounts we own or manage (Facebook Pages, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube).

What data the App accesses

  • Basic profile information of the connected account (name, username, profile picture, account ID), used to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by each platform, used only to publish our own posts and read their performance statistics.
  • Post performance metrics (views, likes, comments, followers) for our own accounts.
  • On YouTube: our channel's basic information, the videos and thumbnails we upload, and our channel's analytics, accessed through YouTube API Services.

How data is used

Data is used solely to schedule and publish our own content and to review how it performs. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer any data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Advertising and third-party content

The App shows no advertising and no third-party content.

Cookies and device storage

The App stores a login cookie in the operator's own browser to keep the session signed in. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies and stores nothing else on users' devices.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a database on infrastructure we control, reachable only by us, and access tokens are kept only for as long as an account remains connected.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the App deletes its tokens. You can also revoke access at any time from the platform's own settings (Facebook: Settings → Business Integrations; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions). To request deletion of any data, see our data deletion instructions.

YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) also applies. You can revoke the App's access to your Google/YouTube data at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
