Data Deletion Instructions

The App at https://postiz.taila5d151.ts.net stores only the access tokens and basic profile information needed to publish to accounts we own or manage.

To delete your data

  1. Remove the App's access from your account: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → remove "Content Studio". TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Google → myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Open a request at https://github.com/ViduraMC/legal/issues/new titled "Data deletion request" with your account username only (requests are public, so include nothing private). We delete all stored data for that account within 30 days and confirm in the request.
